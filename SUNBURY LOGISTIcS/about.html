About Sunbury Logistics

Moving Value, Not Just Cargo.

WHO WE ARE

Connecting Businesses. Moving Possibilities.

Sunbury Logistics is a logistics and transportation company based in Nakawa, Kampala, Uganda.

We focus on providing integrated logistics solutions through air freight, sea freight, road transportation and clearing and forwarding.

Our objective is to simplify cargo movement and help businesses connect with suppliers, customers and markets across the world.

Our Mission

To provide dependable, efficient and professional logistics solutions that simplify transportation, connect markets and support business growth.

Our Vision

To become a trusted logistics partner, recognised for connecting businesses and destinations through innovative transportation and cargo solutions.

Our Values

Professionalism, reliability, transparency, customer commitment and continuous improvement guide our approach to logistics.

OUR COMMITMENT

Every Shipment Matters.

At Sunbury Logistics, we understand that every shipment represents an important business commitment. We strive to deliver logistics solutions that give our customers confidence throughout their cargo journey.
